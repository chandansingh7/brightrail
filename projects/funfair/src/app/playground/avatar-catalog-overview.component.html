Tooltips on avatars

Use tooltip and related inputs on brightrail-avatar for roster hints, roster overflow, and profile summaries without wrapping extra elements.

Example markup

<!-- Profile row (compose avatar + your card markup) -->
<brightrail-avatar kind="image" shape="circle" size="lg" imageSrc="/images/avatar/avatar-02.png" imageAlt="Olivia Rhye" borderStyle="subtle" />

<!-- Centered label under avatar -->
<brightrail-avatar kind="image" shape="circle" size="lg" imageSrc="..." imageAlt="Sophia" label="Sophia Carter" subtitle="Product designer" status="online" [showProfileMeta]="true" />

<!-- Enterprise -->
<brightrail-avatar kind="image" shape="circle" size="md" imageSrc="..." enterpriseRole="assignee" />
<brightrail-avatar kind="image" shape="circle" size="md" imageSrc="..." enterpriseRole="reviewer" />

<!-- Group + overflow -->
<brightrail-avatar-group maxVisible="3" ariaLabel="Team">
  <brightrail-avatar kind="image" shape="circle" size="md" imageSrc="..." imageAlt="A" />
  <brightrail-avatar kind="initials" name="Ben Carter" shape="circle" size="md" tone="success" />
  ...
</brightrail-avatar-group>

<!-- Futuristic -->
<brightrail-avatar kind="image" variant="neon" status="online" glowColor="#22d3ee" ... />
<brightrail-avatar kind="icon" icon="user" variant="ai-assistant" ... />
